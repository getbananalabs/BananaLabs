§ Privacy policy

We collect nothing.

The plugin and the tool run on your machine. They send nothing to Banana Labs: no analytics, no telemetry, no accounts.

01 The only remote calls

  • Your own Framer project, through the Framer Server API, read only.
  • Your own hosting, where the copy is deployed.
  • Your own S3 or R2 storage, only when you configure it.

Form flows send the test values from your own configuration to your own forms endpoint, and to Framer only when you ask for it.

02 Keys and this site

Keys and tokens stay in your local config; the tool never copies, logs or reports them. This site uses no cookies and no tracking. Support requests go through our helpdesk, and what you send there is used only to answer you.
