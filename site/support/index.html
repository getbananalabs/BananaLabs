§ Support

Something broke? Tell us.

Two ways in. Both reach the people who build the tool.

01 Helpdesk

helpdesk.twogether.one/submit

For anything you would rather not post in public. Attach files there.

02 GitHub Issues

github.com/getbananalabs/BananaLabs/issues

Best for bugs. Public, searchable.

01 What to include

  1. The tool version: bananamigrator --version
  2. The site name or published URL.
  3. The command you ran and its console output.
  4. The report.json or flows.json from the run. Remove anything private before you attach it, and never send your .env.
